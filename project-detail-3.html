Cooper Designs

2026 · Full-Stack Engineer · Next.js · HTML · CSS · JavaScript

Cooper Designs is a web design business I started with my childhood friend Alec Wright. Alec is an architect who understands both physical and graphic design, so we make a good team. It started as a side project to help close friends and family get an online presence, but it picked up faster than we expected and now we have new clients coming in regularly.

My side of the work is all full-stack. I handle everything from initial design mockups to deployment. Most of our sites are built on Next.js and hosted through Cloudflare, which keeps things fast and cheap to maintain. Each site is built from scratch to match what the client actually needs rather than dropping them into a template.

The business is still growing and we are taking on new clients. If you need a website built, reach out.
