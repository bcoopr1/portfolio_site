What you're looking at

    
    
      
  • Five depth layers Sky, two far ridges, near ridge, valley floor — each fades with distance (aerial perspective).
    • 
      
  • Move your mouse The layers parallax at different rates. Scroll does it too.
    • 
      
  • Five times of day Dawn, day, golden hour, dusk, night — auto-synced to the visitor's clock. Try the switcher, bottom right.
    • 
      
  • Sun & moon Live in the sky layer itself, so the ridges truly occlude them — the golden-hour sun sits half-set behind the peaks.
    • 
      
  • Procedural terrain The ridgelines are generated fresh on every visit. No two mountains are the same.
    • 
      
  • The river Two counter-flowing currents, sun glints by day, a moonglade at night.
    • 
      
  • The cabin Finds the flattest spot on the near ridge and settles there, chimney smoke and all.
    • 
      
  • Night sky Twinkling starfield and the Big Dipper — upper right.
    • 
      
  • Click the night sky Shooting star. Click during the day: a flock of birds in V formation.
    • 
      
  • Wind Watch the valley grass — gusts ripple through it.
    • 
      
  • Still flying The drone patrol and birds from the original stay, now with flocks.
    • 
      
  • Good citizen Honors prefers-reduced-motion, pauses when the tab is hidden, no GSAP needed for any of it.
    • 
    

    
If you like it, the engine drops into js/main.js and the switcher HUD goes away — the scene just follows the real clock.
