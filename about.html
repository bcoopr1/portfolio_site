About

Background & experience

I'm a full-stack developer with seven years of experience building software that people actually use. I started my career at Thornfield Digital, a boutique agency in Boston where I learned to move fast and deliver under pressure. That environment taught me that good engineering and good communication are inseparable.

After three years at the agency, I joined Clearwater Finance as a senior engineer, where I led the rebuild of their core reporting infrastructure — migrating from a legacy Rails monolith to a modern Next.js frontend backed by a Go API and PostgreSQL. That project cut report generation time from 40 seconds to under 800ms and is now used by over 200 financial advisors daily.

In 2023 I went independent. I work with early-stage startups and established companies alike — typically taking on projects where the engineering challenge and the product challenge are genuinely intertwined. I care about the full picture: architecture, performance, developer experience, and the small interaction details that make something feel right rather than merely functional.

I'm currently based in Texas, though I work with teams across Europe and North America. I'm always open to interesting conversations about new projects, consulting engagements, or just trading notes on TypeScript and distributed systems.
